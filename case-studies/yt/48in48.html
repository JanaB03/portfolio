UB Healthy Project

A food-equity nonprofit needed a full digital presence in 48 hours. We shipped it. We won first place out of 50 teams.

RoleLead designer · cross-functional
Year2024
Build time48 hours · 50 competing teams · 1st place

↑ ubhealthyproject.org — still live

01 Discovery

Four hours to learn the whole organization.

The opening session was a stakeholder interview with the nonprofit's founder. Four hours to understand who they serve, what they need from a website right now, and what they wish they could do but can't afford. The brief got cut down on the spot — only the things that would make a difference on Monday morning.

02 Design

Calm palette. Big type. Older-user-first.

The volunteer base and audience skew older. So: oversized type, deep contrast, no decorative animation, big tap targets. Palette pulled from food — leafy greens, warm golden yellows. Friendly without being childish.

Tradeoff: skipped the slick hero animation everyone wanted in the kickoff. The site reads instead of performs.

03 Build

Decisions made fast. Decisions stuck.

Cross-functional team — I led design while devs built in parallel. The hack: every design decision had a 10-minute fuse. If we couldn't agree in 10 minutes, the lead designer (me) called it and we moved on.

Zero revisits. No "what if we changed the nav at 3am". Velocity over optimization.

04 Outcome

1st place. Site still live.

50 teams. We won. More importantly the nonprofit got a real website out of the weekend — not a deck, not a Figma file, an actual ubhealthyproject.org they can hand to grant-writers and program staff. Best lesson of the year on what "design done" can mean when the deadline is hard.
