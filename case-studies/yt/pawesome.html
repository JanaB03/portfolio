Pawesome

A pet-care app for the everyday small joys of having a pet — sitemap to hi-fi prototype, designed and coded end to end.

RoleDesigner · developer (solo)
YearSummer 2025

↑ Pawesome — designed around the moments, not the metrics

01 Research

The unmet need was sharing, not tracking.

Talked to friends with pets. Asked what they actually open their pet apps for. Most never opened them — they used group chats and camera roll. Tracking apps felt like work. The dopamine was in showing off the dog to a friend.

So Pawesome leaned into the moments, not the metrics.

02 Design

Warm palette, rounded everything, friendly type.

Designed the whole IA on paper first — sitemap, then flows for the three "small joy" moments (a walk, a vet trip, a milestone). Hi-fi in Figma with components named like they're characters in a children's book.

Used the prototype as a sales tool for the build — every screen had to earn its place by being a moment I'd want to show someone.

03 Build

Coded the main flows myself.

First project where I built what I designed. The gap between "Figma green-yellow looks great" and "CSS green-yellow looks dingy" was educational. Caught a dozen design mistakes that only show up when you have to type them.

04 Reflection

Designing AND building is a different relationship.

When you only design, you hand off and wait. When you build what you design, the feedback loop is hours instead of weeks — and you stop pretending edge cases are "engineering's problem."

Pawesome taught me to design like the person building it has to live with my decisions. Because that person was me.
