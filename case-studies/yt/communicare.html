@janabridi TL;DR 2 weeks ago

CommuniCare is a communication platform for residents at Father Joe's Villages — the largest provider of homeless services in San Diego. It had to work on any device that walks in the door: shared kiosks, donated Androids, the case manager's iPhone, an old laptop in the resource room.

This was the most meaningful design work I've done. Field-built, end to end.

@janabridi Research 2 weeks ago

Designing where the users actually are.

We spent time at Father Joe's. Sat in waiting rooms. Watched what "communication" actually looks like when you don't always have a phone, don't always have charge, and the people you need to reach are intermittent.

Found that the biggest unmet need wasn't "how to send messages" — it was trust that the message got through. That reframed half the product.

02
@janabridi Design 2 weeks ago

Designed for the lowest-common-denominator device.

Big touch targets. High contrast. No-account-required flows. Offline-friendly screens so the app doesn't break when the wifi blinks. Type sized for older users and low-light kiosks.

Tested every screen with actual residents. Every iteration came back with "this word means something else here" — language was a bigger design problem than layout.

03
@janabridi Build 2 weeks ago

Worked alongside engineers and field staff.

Tight loop with the dev team — design decisions reviewed at the same table as build constraints. Case managers tested screens in their actual workflows; their feedback closed bugs the dev team would never have seen.

04
@janabridi Outcome 2 weeks ago

Shipped. In use.

Now running across Father Joe's programs. The most humbling part of the work was realizing that "shipped" was a milestone, not the finish — every new resident reveals an edge case we hadn't designed for.

Equal parts humbling, hard, and the most meaningful design work I've done.
