About

jana bridi · san diego

The engineer who designs how people think.

I'm a Cog Sci grad from UCSD with a soft spot for experiences that just feel right. I design from the user up — translating how people actually behave into interfaces that read naturally.

Most weeks I'm somewhere between a Figma file, a Claude Code window, and a notebook full of half-formed sketches. I love the moment where a design idea becomes a thing you can touch.

Open to design and design-engineering roles. Field work, weekend prototypes, and full AI builds especially welcome.
